# WhatsApp message drafts (English)

Placeholders in {curly brackets} are filled in by the app.

## Draft 1 — Fee reminder to a parent who has NOT subscribed
Sent automatically when fees are due, and when the teacher taps **Remind**.

> Hello! This is a reminder from {Teacher name}'s {Class name} class.
> {Student name}'s {Month} fees are due.
>
> 📱 Want to see {Student name}'s attendance, marks and progress every day?
> Subscribe to Plus: {join link}

## Draft 2 — Invite (teacher sends to parents / students)
Sent from the **Digital invite** screen (Send on WhatsApp).

> Hi! {Teacher name}'s {Class name} class now uses Plus.
> Join to see attendance, marks and class updates: {join link}

## Draft 3 — "Remind" from the By-class list (several parents at once)
Same wording as Draft 1, sent one message per parent who has not subscribed.

## Notes
- Keep messages short and polite. No amounts in the promo line.
- Tamil and Sinhala versions will be added later.
- WhatsApp needs approved templates for messages that start a conversation (see README §8 of the app). Until approved, the app's `wa.me` fallback works.
